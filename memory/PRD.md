## Target Market
- **Primary**: Visakhapatnam (Vizag) and surrounding areas
- **Secondary**: Rajahmundry, Kakinada, Srikakulam, Vijayanagram
- **Segments**: All budget ranges (₹30L - ₹1.5Cr+) with 3-tier packages

## Service Mix (Strategic Priority)
1. **Home Construction (60%)** - Primary revenue driver
   - Villa Construction
   - Duplex Homes
   - Individual Houses (1-4 BHK)
   - Renovations & Extensions

2. **Interior Design (40%)** - Premium add-on
   - Included in Luxury package
   - Available as standalone service
   - Modular kitchens, wardrobes, complete home interiors

3. **Hidden Strengths** (Not over-emphasized)
   - BIM Technology & 3D Modeling
   - Architecture & Planning
   - Structural Engineering

## Competitive Differentiators
1. **Technology-Driven**: BIM modeling, 3D visualization, digital quality checks
2. **Transparent Pricing**: Fixed-price packages with detailed quotations
3. **Safe Payments**: Milestone-based, no advance to contractors
4. **On-Time Delivery**: 6-8 months average with tracking app
5. **10-Year Warranty**: Structural warranty on all projects
